SAQI

ساقي · الإنذار المبكر لمصادر المياه

An AI early-warning system that tells herders, village councils and water authorities a desert well is failing weeks before it runs dry.

How SAQI works

Every night, signals that are each weak on their own are fused into one forecast with an honest confidence range, then turned into a message a herder with a basic phone can act on.
01 · SENSE

Pull the data

Groundwater, vegetation, rain and climate layers from Google Earth Engine, plus well sensors and herder reports.

GRACE-FOSentinel-2Sentinel-1CHIRPSERA5
02 · CLEAN

Handle the mess

Cloudy pixels are filled in from radar. A sensor that goes silent for 48 hours is dropped, and the confidence range widens to match.

03 · PREDICT

Fuse and forecast

A Kalman filter combines the signals into one water-level estimate, then a survival model predicts the days left, with an 80% range.

KalmanLightGBMquantiles
04 · DECIDE

Pick the alert

There are four alert levels. A Warning only goes out when two independent signals agree.

05 · WARN

Reach people

Arabic SMS and an automated voice call to basic phones, a WhatsApp message to councils, and a dashboard for the water authority. Herders reply 1 or 2, and their replies feed back into the model.

⌁ When a sensor dies

Sweihan's sensor has been silent for 6 days. SAQI switches to satellite data and herder reports, widens the forecast range from ±9 to ±35 days, labels the forecast DEGRADED, and raises a repair ticket.

☁ When the image is cloudy

Sentinel-1 radar sees through cloud, and the last clear 10-day composite fills any gaps. GRACE groundwater data is never blocked by cloud.

⚖ Trust vs. lives

Early Watch notices go out easily because they cost little. Costly actions such as rationing or sending trucks need two signals to agree. Each well's false-alarm rate is public.

STABLE

P(fail ≤ 8w) < 20%

Dashboard only, plus a weekly digest.

WATCH

P(fail ≤ 8w) ≥ 20%

The council gets a heads-up and herders are asked to report what they see.

WARNING

P(fail ≤ 6w) ≥ 50% · 2 signals

SMS and a voice call to herders, a suggestion for another well, and a rationing plan.

ACT NOW

P(fail ≤ 3w) ≥ 70% · 2 signals

Water truck routes go out, herds are moved, and pumping is rotated between wells.
