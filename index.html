From satellite to SMS

The full chain runs every night. Weak signals that are each unreliable on their own are fused into one forecast with an honest confidence range, then turned into a message a herder with a basic phone can act on.

01 · SENSE

Pull the data

Groundwater, vegetation, rain and climate layers from Google Earth Engine, plus well sensors and herder reports.

GRACE-FOSentinel-2Sentinel-1 SARCHIRPSERA5IoT level
02 · CLEAN

Handle the mess

Cloudy pixels masked and gap-filled with radar. Dead sensors detected (flat-line or silence for 48 h) and dropped from the fusion, with the confidence range widened to match.

03 · PREDICT

Fuse and forecast

A Kalman filter merges the signals into one water-level state. A gradient-boosted survival model then predicts days until the well fails, with an 80% interval.

Kalman fusionLightGBM survivalquantile CI
04 · DECIDE

Pick the alert level

Four rungs from Watch to Act. A Warning only fires when two independent signals agree, which keeps false alarms down without missing real failures.

05 · WARN

Reach people

Arabic SMS and an automated voice call to basic phones, WhatsApp to village councils, and a dashboard for the authority. Herders reply 1 or 2 to confirm, which feeds the model.

Built for the desert rules

Every design choice answers one of the three constraints in the brief.

⌁

When a sensor dies

Sweihan's level sensor has been silent for 6 days. SAQI does not go quiet: it switches to satellite and herder reports, widens the forecast range from ±9 to ±35 days, and shows a DEGRADED label so nobody over-trusts it. A maintenance ticket is also raised.

☁

When the image is cloudy

If a Sentinel-2 vegetation image is cloudy, SAQI uses Sentinel-1 radar (which sees through cloud) and the last clear 10-day composite instead. Monthly GRACE groundwater data is never blocked by cloud, so it anchors the slow trend.

⚖

Trust vs. lives

A missed alarm can cost a herd; a false alarm costs trust. So the cheap, early Watch notice fires easily, while costly actions like rationing or trucks need two independent signals to agree. Each well's false-alarm rate is shown publicly.

The alert ladder

Thresholds use the probability that the well fails within a time window, so the message always says how sure we are.

STABLE

P(fail ≤ 8w) < 20%

Dashboard only. Weekly digest to the water authority.

WATCH

P(fail ≤ 8w) ≥ 20%

One signal is enough. The council gets a heads-up and herders are asked to report what they see.

WARNING

P(fail ≤ 6w) ≥ 50% · 2 signals agree

Arabic SMS and voice call to registered herders, an alternative well suggested, and a rationing plan sent to the council.

ACT

P(fail ≤ 3w) ≥ 70% · 2 signals agree

Aid agency gets truck routing, herds are moved to the nearest stable source, and pumping is rotated.

Who gets warned, and how

One forecast is shaped into four different messages, each for a different person.

HERDER · BASIC PHONE

SMS + voice call in Arabic

Under 160 characters, no app, no data plan. Says where to go next and when. The voice call covers anyone who cannot read.

VILLAGE COUNCIL

WhatsApp or SMS brief

A rationing plan and herd rotation schedule, plus which neighbouring well can share water.

WATER AUTHORITY

Hologram dashboard

500+ wells ranked by days left, with drill-down to every signal and a log of past alerts.

AID AGENCY

Truck routing

Which oases need water trucks in the next 3 weeks, and the shortest route between them.
